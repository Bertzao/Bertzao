Your Rio.

We were born here, we grew up here, and we still live here. Rocinha isn't a stop on our route — it's home, and everyone in the alleyways knows us by name.

That's why we only take one group at a time. No vans, no headsets, no crowd of thirty people photographing rooftops from a jeep. Just you and us, walking at your pace, stopping where you want, eating where we eat, meeting the people we grew up with.

Part of every experience goes to Acorda Capoeira, the social project that teaches capoeira to kids in Rocinha. Coming with us puts money straight into the community you're visiting.

What you actually get

No small print, no surprise add-ons at the meeting point. This is what every booking includes.

  • Your group only — 6 to 8 people, never more

    Book for two and it's the two of you. Book for six and it's your six. We never merge bookings to fill a slot, which is the whole reason we don't run vans.

  • One of us with you the whole time

    No handoff to a driver, no meeting a stranger halfway. The cousin who answers your message is the cousin who walks with you.

  • The moto-taxi rides are part of it

    Getting up the hill is half the fun and it's already included — you're not paying extra at the bottom of the road.

  • Registered with Favela Turismo

    Every tour we run is logged in the Favela Turismo app, the official register for tourism inside the community.

Questions

Is it safe?

Yes. We live in Rocinha and we know every corner and every person on the way. You walk with locals who are respected here, we go only where and when it's calm, and we stay with you the whole time. Thousands of travellers have done this with us without a single problem.

How do I book?

Message us on Instagram or WhatsApp. Tell us which experience you want, your dates and how many people. We'll answer with everything you need to know and confirm your spot in the same conversation.

Where do we meet?

At São Conrado metro station, Exit A, right next to the Rocinha street market. It's easy to reach from Copacabana, Ipanema and Leblon, and we'll send you the exact pin when you book.

Which languages do you speak?

English, Spanish and Portuguese — we grew up switching between the three. This page is also translated into French so you can read it comfortably, but the tour itself is guided in English, Spanish or Portuguese. Write to us in whichever of those three works best for you.
