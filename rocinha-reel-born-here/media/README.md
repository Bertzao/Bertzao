Tips
- Shoot vertical, highest quality, never pass files through WhatsApp.
- Pick the in-point of each clip in `slots.json` (`"in": 4.5` = start at 4.5 s) and nudge the crop with `"focus"`.
- For `cousins`: faces in the upper half of the frame; text sits in the lower half.
- Anyone recognizable besides you two (guests, Mestre Manel, neighbors) needs to have said yes to appearing.
